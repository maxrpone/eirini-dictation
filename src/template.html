For Mum: how to use this
  1. The voice speaks Putonghua (普通話), the same as the teacher.
  2. Tap Mock test. Tap the big yellow button. Listen to the voice.
  3. Eirini writes on paper, or with her finger in the box.
  4. Long sentences come in small parts, like the teacher reads them. Use the arrows for the next part. The voice also says the punctuation (逗號 = comma, 句號 = full stop).
  5. Tap Show answer. Compare the characters one by one. Then tap the number of mistakes. The game counts the marks.
  6. Use headphones on the plane. Tap Slow if the voice is too fast.
  7. On iPhone, if you hear nothing, turn off silent mode (the switch on the side) and turn the volume up.
  • Part 甲 (30 marks): 10 of these 13 words. 3 marks for each word.
  • Part 乙 (60 marks): Lesson 10, paragraphs 5, 6, 8 and 9. Each mistake loses 3 marks.
  • Part 丙 (10 marks): two extra sentences (課外一, 課外二). Each mistake loses 1 mark.
  • Part 丁, bonus (20 marks): idioms, book 2, pages 9 to 14. The test gives a list of idioms. Eirini writes the correct idiom in each sentence. 4 marks for each. The teacher wants Eirini to read the 2 example sentences for each idiom in the book. The sentences in this game are new practice sentences. They are not the sentences in the book.
